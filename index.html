What it is

An audit, not a guard.

You run Pickoff on your own machine when you want a check. It reads how your AI agents are set up and reports the paths an attacker could use to reach your keys. It does not watch your agents or block anything while they work.

What it does today

  • Finds the AI agents and tools on your machine: Claude, Codex, Cursor, Grok Bot
  • Finds the keys they can reach: plain-text tokens, SSH keys, .env files
  • Draws each path: untrusted text → agent → key → your data
  • Applies the simple fixes for you, like locking a loose key file, with a backup and an undo. The rest come with steps.

Not built yet

  • Watching your agents while they run
  • Holding a risky action before it happens
  • Text alerts you answer with one word
  • Replaying an attack against your own setup

The demo on this page is scripted, on a fictional company. It shows the full idea, including the parts that are not built yet.

Trust

Your stack, your rules.

A security tool that reads your setup should say exactly what it does there.

01

Read-only

The scan only reads. Nothing changes until you say so.

02

Runs on your machine

The scan and the dashboard run locally, on your own computer.

03

Secrets stay masked

Pickoff never prints or uploads a secret’s value. Findings show a masked preview only.

04

Runs only when you run it

Pickoff is an audit, not a background service. Quit it and nothing keeps running.

05

Every fix has an undo

Each fix keeps a backup, and one click restores the original file.
